## Washington Hate Week
The Ducks are still alive, the standings have tightened once again, and Rivalry Week arrives with everything still on the line.

A month ago, Oregon's season felt like it was hanging by a thread.The Ducks were being written off by national media, playoff discussions had quieted down, and the path to Indianapolis looked impossible. Fast forward to today, and suddenly Oregon finds itself with a shot to play for a Big Ten Championship and potentially punch a ticket to the College Football Playoff.

Funny how quickly things change when you fire a coordinator.

But before anyone starts booking postseason travel, there is one very important obstacle standing in the way:

Washington.

Or, as it's known around here...

 **Washington Hate Week**

## Rivalry Week has arrived.

The Ducks enter the final week of the regular season knowing exactly what's at stake. A win over Washington keeps the dream alive. A loss ends any discussion about conference championships or playoff scenarios.

The mission is simple:

Beat Washington.

Unfortunately, Oregon's fate is not entirely in its own hands.

Oregon Misses The Conference Championship If:
Indiana loses to Purdue,
UCLA loses to USC ,
& Iowa beats Nebraska

The Ducks need help, but for the first time in weeks, that help feels realistic.

Now Oregon just has to handle its business.

And let's be honest, beating Washington always feels good.

### 🏆 FEBU Standings Update

Just when it looked like Sco might finally create some separation, college football did what college football does best:

Completely lose its mind.

Upsets were everywhere, favorites fell, predictions were ruined, and nearly everyone in FEBU had a rough week trying to make sense of the chaos.

The biggest beneficiary?

Kellz.

While nobody exactly dominated the week, Kellz managed to claw back valuable points and once again pull even with Sco atop the standings.

Current Situation

🥇 Sco - Tied for 1st

🥇 Kellz - Tied for 1st

🥉 Lil Jev - Tied for 3rd

🥉 talesofderp - Tied for 3rd

With Rivalry Week now upon us, there may not be another opportunity this season to make major gains in the standings.

Every pick matters.

Every upset matters.

Every bonus point matters.

And with so many rivalry games known for producing absolute nonsense, anything can happen.

### 🎯 Bonus Point Watch

Sco nearly doubled up on bonus points this week.

His Oregon score prediction was the closest among the group and earned him the weekly bonus point.

Perhaps more impressive?

Sco finished just one point away from earning a second bonus point.

A razor-thin margin that could become significant if this race remains deadlocked heading into the final weeks.

In a season this competitive, every single point feels enormous.

### 🌯 Chaos Report

The standings remain tight.

The playoff picture remains messy.

The Ducks are still alive.

Washington Week is here.

And for one final regular-season weekend, everyone still has something to play for.

Whether you're chasing a playoff berth, a conference championship, first place in FEBU, or simply the opportunity to beat Washington, the stakes could not be much higher.
