## 🏈 Welcome to the Madness

College football season is back, and so are we  the loud, chaotic, overly‑invested fans who treat every Saturday like a national holiday. This site is our little corner of the internet, built for one purpose: **to track our predictions, argue about them, and enjoy the beautiful nonsense that comes with being a fan.**

Every week, we’ll drop a fresh prediction form so you can lock in your picks. Conference games, big matchups, chaos alerts — whatever the week throws at us, we’re logging it. As the season rolls on, we’ll keep an **overall standings board** to see who’s actually good at predicting things… and who should probably never be trusted again.

We’ll also occasionally post whatever fandom‑related chaos pops up — rants, hype moments, questionable takes, or anything else that deserves a spot on the page. Nothing too serious. Just the kind of stuff fans yell about in group chats anyway.

A new season means new drama, new contenders, and new opportunities to be incredibly wrong. Let’s get into it.
