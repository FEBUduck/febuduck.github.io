### It was a Saturday to forget for Oregon.

The Ducks were handed a major defeat by Oklahoma State, and there weren't many if any positives to take away from the performance. From top to bottom, Oregon looked terrible, with the entire team underperforming in a game that they should have won going away.

Now, the focus shifts to a crucial bounce-back opportunity against Portland State this Friday night.While the matchup may not carry the same national spotlight, it presents Oregon with a chance to regroup, clean up mistakes, and regain some confidence before a much bigger challenge looms on the horizon. The following week brings a showdown with USC, a game that could have significant implications moving forward.

### Pick'em Standings Remain Tight

The Oregon loss also had a major impact on this week's pick'em standings, although not in the way many expected.
Because every participant predicted an Oregon victory, no additional points were awarded this week. The result left the standings largely unchanged, but the race at the top continues to heat up. Kellz has continued an impressive surge and has now pulled even with Tyler, creating a tie for first place.
Looking across the leaderboard, consistency has been the story so far. Most participants are currently hitting on 75% or more of their selections, keeping the competition incredibly close through the early portion of the season.

With Big Ten conference play right around the corner, the schedule is set to become far less predictable. Tougher matchups and higher-stakes games are likely to create more disagreement , giving competitors a chance for everyone to separate themselves from the pack. The margin for error will shrink, and every prediction will carry additional weight.
