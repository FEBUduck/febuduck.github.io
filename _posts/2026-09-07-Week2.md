### Week 1 is in the books

The Ducks survived Boise State in a game that was a little closer than most Oregon fans would have preferred. A win is a win, but there are definitely some things to clean up before Oklahoma State comes to town.

On the prediction front, Tyler earned two bonus points by coming closest to Oregon's final score. Congratulations to Tyler for proving that blindly guessing numbers is, in fact, a legitimate strategy.

Ohio State vs. Texas is the headliner and could have major playoff implications before conference play even gets rolling. Michigan vs. Oklahoma gives us another heavyweight matchup, and there are enough tricky games across the slate to make everyone question their picks at least twice.
