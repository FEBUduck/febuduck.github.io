## 🏈 Prediction Rules

Here’s how our weekly prediction system works:

### **1. Weekly B1G Game Picks**
Every week, we post all Big Ten matchups.  
For each game, you simply pick the winner.

- **Correct pick = 1 point**

Nice and simple.

### **2. The Duck Game (Bonus Points)**
For the Oregon Ducks game, things get a little more interesting.  
In addition to picking the winner, you also predict the **final score**.

Points for the Duck game:

- **Correct winner = 1 point**  
- **Closest to the final score of the Duck game = 1 bonus point**

Closest score is determined by total point differential from the actual final score.

### **3. Weekly Standings**
After each week, points are added to the season total.  
We keep a running **overall standings board** so you can see:

- Who’s on fire  
- Who’s barely hanging on  
- Who’s completely guessing

### **4. No Edits After Kickoff**
Once the games begin, predictions are locked in.  
Make sure you submit your picks before kickoff each week.
All picks are timestamped

---

These rules keep things simple, competitive, and chaotic — just the way we like it.
